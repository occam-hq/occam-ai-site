In daily use

Our own platform

Occam HQ, the system that runs Occam AI

We wanted one place to run the whole company: clients, projects, meetings, email, documents, billing, and the books. Nothing on the market fit how we work, so we built it, with an AI assistant that works across every part of it. We run on it every day.

These screens are from our demo copy of HQ, filled with made-up clients.

Clients

Everything about a client, on one page

Contacts, projects, meetings, documents, email, invoices, and tasks are all linked to the client they belong to. Open a client and the whole relationship is there: what we’re building, what’s been billed and paid, when we next meet, and what we owe them.

  • A history of every change, field by field
  • Tasks with owners and due dates
  • Contract value, invoiced, and paid at a glance

Projects

Projects that know what they’re owed

Every project is a series of stages, each with dates and a price. When a stage ends, its bill is ready to invoice, and when the client pays, the stage shows it. One look tells us what’s done, what’s due, and what hasn’t been invoiced yet.

  • One-time, monthly, and contingent charges
  • The code repository, app, and test site linked from the header
  • Documents, meetings, and email for the project, in its tabs

Meetings

Meetings that file their own notes

We book calls from HQ, and it creates the Zoom link and sends the calendar invite. After the call, the notes and transcript land on the meeting, under the right client, with the decisions and action items pulled out. Nobody types up a summary or wonders where it went.

  • Our Google Calendar synced in, with holidays and due dates alongside
  • Each meeting matched to its client by who attended
  • Rescheduling moves the Zoom meeting and the invite with it

Email

Email, filed where it belongs

Our Gmail syncs in, and each thread is filed under the client and project it’s about, so the conversation sits next to the work. We read, reply, and forward from the same screen, and an AI writer drafts or polishes a reply from a line of instructions.

  • Threads matched to clients by who’s on them
  • Attachments saved to the client’s documents in one click
  • Notes and activity beside every thread

Billing

From invoice to paid, without a spreadsheet

Invoices are built from what each project has billed, sent from our billing address with a branded email and a PDF, and matched to payments as they arrive from the bank. Overdue invoices surface on their own, and every client has a running account of what they’ve been billed and what they’ve paid.

  • Payments matched to invoices from the bank feed
  • Partial payments and voided invoices handled properly
  • What’s outstanding, overdue, and not yet invoiced, always current

Expenses

The books, kept as we go

Vendors, bills, subscriptions, and every bank and card transaction come into one place and get sorted as they arrive. We always know what came in, what went out, what we spend by category, and what each partner has put in or taken out.

  • New bank transactions waiting to be sorted, never lost
  • Subscriptions tracked with their monthly cost
  • Each partner’s deposits and withdrawals reconciled

The assistant

An assistant that works across all of it

Ask it anything about the business, or tell it what to do: draft an invoice, book a meeting, file a thread, update a project. It reads what it needs from HQ, does the work, and shows what it changed. Anything that sends an email, invites someone, deletes something, or moves money waits for one of us to approve it.

It also thinks before it acts. Asked to book a check-in at a time that was already taken, it stopped, pointed out the clash, and asked what we wanted instead.
