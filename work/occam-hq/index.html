Our own platform

Occam HQ, the system we run Occam AI on

We run our own company on a platform we built: clients, meetings, documents, email, and billing in one place, with an AI assistant that does the work and asks before anything goes out.

Clients, contacts, projects, meetings, documents, email, invoices, payments, expenses, and tasks all live in one place, connected to Gmail, Google Calendar, Zoom, and our bank feed. Every edit is recorded, and any change can be undone.

Most businesses that serve clients need the same core: a record of each client, meetings and notes, documents to sign, email, and billing. HQ is where we build and prove those parts before we bring them to a client, set up around how that business runs.
