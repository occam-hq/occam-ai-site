Home care agency

Case acceptance, around the clock

A system that reads every new patient case posted to the care portals and accepts or declines it by the agency’s own rules, in seconds, day and night.

New patient cases are posted to care-coordination portals, and a case usually goes to the first agency that accepts it. Staff used to watch the portals and click accept or decline by hand. Now the system reads each new case, applies the agency’s own rules, and responds within seconds.

It runs in the agency’s own cloud account, keeps only the few fields it needs to make a decision, and records a full audit trail.

  1. 01Set the rulesStaff choose the areas, languages, rates, and contracts the agency takes, in a control panel they edit themselves
  2. 02Watch the portalsChecks each portal for new cases every few seconds, around the clock or within set hours
  3. 03Read the caseOpens each new case and pulls only the fields that matter: location, language, pay rate, and service type
  4. 04DecideAccepts or declines by the agency’s rules, and leaves anything unclear for a person to review
  5. 05Act and confirmResponds in the portal with the proper reason, then checks that the action actually went through
  6. 06Stay in controlEvery case, decision, and settings change is logged. Staff can switch the system to watch-only or off at any time
