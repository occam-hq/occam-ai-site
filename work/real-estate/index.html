Launching

Real estate brokerage

Every deal, to the buyers it fits

Off-market deals don’t wait, and matching them to buyers from memory meant most never reached the right ones. We built a deal desk that finds the 40 to 50 investors each deal fits and writes to every one of them personally.

The brokerage brings off-market deals to a list of more than 1,200 investors, and off-market deals don’t last. An assistant was typing buyer names into Outlook one deal at a time, follow-ups mostly didn’t happen, and a mass email to a hundred buyers got almost no replies. Blast-email tools hadn’t helped, because the hard part is knowing who a deal is for.

The buyer list was free text, written however someone had typed it. We cleaned it in two passes, first with fixed rules and then with AI reading the rest, and flagged anything uncertain for a person to check. Each new deal, typed in or read from an offering memo, rent roll, or operating statement, is matched against every buyer’s markets, property types, and price, size, and age ranges, with room for near misses. The best fits are ranked with the reasons for each, and buyers who’ve had a lot of deals lately move down, so the same names don’t get every one.

Nothing goes out until the principal approves the list. Then each buyer gets a personal email from his own mailbox, at a human pace, with follow-ups three and seven days later in the same thread. A reply stops the follow-ups. AI reads every reply, marks it interested, pass, or question, and when a buyer says what they’re looking for now, proposes the change to their criteria for one click.
