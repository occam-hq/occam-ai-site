Home care agency

Case acceptance, around the clock

New patient cases are posted to care-coordination portals, and a case usually goes to the first agency that accepts it. Staff used to watch the portals and click accept or decline by hand. Now the system reads each new case, applies the agency’s own rules, and responds within seconds.

It runs in the agency’s own cloud account, keeps only the few fields it needs to make a decision, and records a full audit trail.

  1. 01Set the rulesStaff choose the areas, languages, rates, and contracts the agency takes, in a control panel they edit themselves
  2. 02Watch the portalsChecks each portal for new cases every few seconds, around the clock or within set hours
  3. 03Read the caseOpens each new case and pulls only the fields that matter: location, language, pay rate, and service type
  4. 04DecideAccepts or declines by the agency’s rules, and leaves anything unclear for a person to review
  5. 05Act and confirmResponds in the portal with the proper reason, then checks that the action actually went through
  6. 06Stay in controlEvery case, decision, and settings change is logged. Staff can switch the system to watch-only or off at any time

Accounting firm

From client documents to a return ready for review

Preparing a return means collecting documents, keying in figures, and checking the result against the paperwork. We are building one platform for the firm that tracks each client’s documents, reads them, prepares the draft, and flags anything that doesn’t match. A partner reviews and files every return.

Client data stays in an account the firm controls, and every action is recorded in an audit log.

  1. 01Track the documentsLists what each client is expected to send and shows what is still missing
  2. 02Read the uploadsReads W-2s, 1099s, K-1s, and bank and brokerage statements, and pulls out the figures
  3. 03Analyze the booksCategorizes every bank transaction, flags duplicates and gaps, and compares the year against prior years
  4. 04Draft the returnPrepares a draft return from the documents and the books
  5. 05Check the draftFlags amounts that don’t match a source document, items that are missing, and likely misclassifications, and names the document behind each flag
  6. 06Partner sign-offStaff review the draft, a partner approves it, and a partner always presses submit

Real estate brokerage

Every new deal, to the buyers it fits

A brokerage with a long list of investors gets a steady stream of new deals, and each one has to reach the right buyers before another broker’s does. Matching them by memory and sending them one email at a time is slow, and good deals reach the wrong people. We are building a deal desk that reads each deal, finds the buyers it fits, and keeps track of every reply.

A broker reviews every match before anything goes out.

  1. 01Know the buyersKeeps each buyer’s markets, property types, and price range in one list the team can edit
  2. 02Read the dealPulls the location, property type, price, and key numbers out of each deal as it arrives
  3. 03MatchRanks the buyers whose criteria fit, and says why each one made the list
  4. 04Broker reviewThe broker checks the matches and the email for each buyer before it is sent
  5. 05Track the repliesRecords who is interested and who passed, and why
  6. 06Follow upReminds the team when a buyer is due a follow-up, so no deal goes quiet

About us

The AI team you don’t have to hire

AI can now do a lot of the work that fills a small business’s day. But most owners are too busy running the business to work out where it fits, and hiring engineers to build it is out of reach.

We started Occam AI to close that gap. Joseph learns how your business runs and what it needs; Mayer, who has built AI systems for years, designs and builds the system. You work with both of us, from the plan to the launch.
