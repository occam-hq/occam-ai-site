        Home care agency

        Case acceptance, around the clock

        New patient cases are posted to care-coordination portals, and a case usually goes to the first agency that accepts it. Staff used to watch the portals and click accept or decline by hand. Now the system reads each new case, applies the agency’s own rules, and responds within seconds.

        It runs in the agency’s own cloud account, keeps only the few fields it needs to make a decision, and records a full audit trail.

        Real estate brokerage

        Every new deal, to the buyers it fits

        A brokerage with a long list of investors gets a steady stream of new deals, and each one has to reach the right buyers before another broker’s does. Matching them by memory and sending them one email at a time is slow, and good deals reach the wrong people. We are building a deal desk that reads each deal, finds the buyers it fits, and keeps track of every reply.

        About us

        The AI team you don’t have to hire

        AI can now do a lot of the work that fills a small business’s day. But most owners are too busy running the business to work out where it fits, and hiring engineers to build it is out of reach.

        We started Occam AI to close that gap. Joseph learns how your business runs and what it needs; Mayer, who has built AI systems for years, designs and builds the system. You work with both of us, from the plan to the launch.
